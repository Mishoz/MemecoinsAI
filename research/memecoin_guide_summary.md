# Memecoin Trading Guide Summary
*Source: COMBINED_MEMECOIN_TRADING_REPORT.md*

## 1. Lifecycle & Timing
- **Phase 1: Launch** (0-48h) - Extreme risk, smart money convergence only.
- **Phase 2: Narrative Formation** (2-7 days) - **Primary Target.** Entry on 15-30% dips.
- **Phase 3: Mania** (1-3 weeks) - Exit aggressively.
- **Phase 4: Decay** (Weeks+) - Hard exit.
- **Social Velocity:** Enter 6-18 hours after social momentum starts.

## 2. Position Sizing & Risk
- **Barbell Model:** 90-95% Core, 5-10% Convexity (Memecoins).
- **Position Size:** 0.1-0.5% of total portfolio per trade.
- **Daily Loss Limit:** 3% of budget (Halt trading).
- **Max Concurrent:** 5 open positions.

## 3. Exit Strategies
- **Tiered Take Profit:**
  - TP1 (3-5x): 25%
  - TP2 (8-15x): 25%
  - TP3 (Trailing): 50%
- **Hard Exits:** Developer sells, LP drops >20% in 1h, Social velocity <50%.

## 4. Rug Pull Detection
- **Contract Checks:** Mint authority, Freeze authority, LP lock, Bubblemaps (clusters), Honeypot test.
- **Metrics:** Vol/Liq Ratio (<2.0), Liq/MC Ratio (>5%), Holder Growth (>10% daily).

## 5. Bot Configuration
- **Slippage:** 2-3% (Standard), 3-5% (New pools).
- **MEV Protection:** Mandatory for both Solana (Jito/Helius) and EVM (Flashbots).
- **Time-Based Exits:** 72h rule (No 50% gain -> Cut 50%).

## 6. Tax Optimization
- **Loss Harvesting:** Sell losers to claim deductions; carry forward excess losses.
- **Wash Sale:** No crypto wash sale rule (immediate re-buy allowed).
